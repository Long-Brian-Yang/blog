# Class of 2029 Job Search Roadmap

**Planning started:** 2026-10-27. Company-specific hiring calendars are unverified. Early selection, rolling recruitment, and doctoral hiring differ by employer.

## First 14 days (Oct 27–Nov 9, 2026)
- [ ] Answer self-assessment prompts and draft three experience stories
- [ ] Read two real job postings for each of the four career paths
- [ ] Tentatively list 10–15 interesting employers
- [ ] Check doctoral and graduation-year eligibility
- [ ] Outline a one-page research summary
- [ ] Reflect on interests, questions, and next actions at week's end

## Nov–Dec 2026: Build foundations
- [ ] Populate the industry and role comparison
- [ ] Draft 30-second, 2-minute, and 5-minute research introductions in Japanese and English
- [ ] Understand application essays, aptitude tests, and interview formats
- [ ] Check employer events, lab visits, and internships regularly

## 2027: Gather evidence
- [ ] Explore internships, research events, and doctoral recruiting programs; apply when eligible
- [ ] Talk to employees and researchers about daily work
- [ ] Organize a technical portfolio and research achievements
- [ ] Reassess career hypotheses every six months

## 2028: Apply and interview
- [ ] Verify official Class of 2029 eligibility and deadlines by employer
- [ ] Prepare application essays, CV, research summary, and any references required
- [ ] Practice technical, behavioral, and case interviews and aptitude tests as relevant
- [ ] Track applications and lessons without publishing confidential information

## 2029: Decide and prepare
- [ ] Compare offers, role, placement, location, and development opportunities
- [ ] Confirm degree completion and any necessary administrative procedures

## Sustainable weekly rhythm
Monday: 15 minutes of vacancy/event checks; Wednesday: 30 minutes of reflection or role research; weekend: 30-minute log review and next-week planning. Adapt around research commitments.

## Decision checkpoints
End 2026: articulate interests; mid-2027: test with real experiences; end 2027: provisional application criteria; 2028: revise based on recruiting outcomes.
