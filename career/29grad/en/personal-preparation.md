## 4. Introduction scripts
### 30 seconds — general audience
“I am a doctoral researcher at [institution] studying materials through computational science. I use [methods actually employed] to investigate [problem]. I am exploring how my experience in modeling complex phenomena and validating results could contribute to corporate R&D or scientific computing.”

### 90 seconds — technical audience
“My research focuses on [topic], motivated by [scientific or industrial problem]. I use [DFT/AIMD or actual methods] to investigate [system]. One important challenge was [challenge], which I addressed through [personal action]. I found [verified result], while recognizing [limitation]. I hope to apply this experience in [role].”

### Five-minute technical presentation
Problem (45 s) → prior work and gap (45 s) → method and personal contribution (90 s) → validation and results (90 s) → limitations and relevance (30 s). Prepare at most one useful figure per segment.
