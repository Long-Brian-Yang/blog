## Due diligence checklist
1. Official company website, legal entity, current products, and financing
2. Actual research, computational science, and AI positions and skill requirements
3. Doctoral student and Class of 2029 eligibility, including April 2029 joining
4. Pay (including overtime and equity), location, remote work, and language
5. Business sustainability, funding, and research-to-product maturity
6. Application channel, hiring contacts, and deadlines
