# Job Search Preparation and Draft Scripts — Class of 2029

> **Personal answers, achievements, and quantitative results are not yet confirmed.** These are editable scripts, not claims to submit as fact.

## 1. Ten self-assessment prompts
1. Do I want to remain a researcher or transfer research skills to another career?
2. Which is most enjoyable: hypothesis building, running calculations, coding, or explaining results?
3. Research autonomy or speed of product impact?
4. How strict is the Tokyo preference: 23 wards, wider Tokyo, or commuting region?
5. How do salary, housing, leave, and remote work rank?
6. Do I want to test AI engineering, data science, or consulting?
7. In which language can I best communicate technical ideas?
8. Large-company stability and benefits versus startup autonomy?
9. What specific achievements demonstrate my strengths?
10. What skills do I want in three years?

For each: provisional answer / supporting experience / uncertainty / how to test.

## 2. 30-second introduction
“Hello, I'm [name], a doctoral researcher at [institution] studying [topic]. I use computational methods such as first-principles calculations and molecular dynamics to investigate [research problem]. Through this work, I've developed skills in modeling complex problems, validating calculations, and interpreting results. I'm exploring both materials and energy R&D and roles involving scientific computing and AI. Thank you for speaking with me.”

## 3. Two-minute research pitch
“My research addresses [scientific or industrial problem]. To understand [specific question], I use [methods actually employed] to study [system]. A key challenge was [modeling or validation issue], which I approached by [your action]. The verified result was [result], suggesting [significance]. An important limitation is [limitation], so my next step is [validation]. I hope to apply this approach to [role-relevant problem].”

## 4. Strength statement
“My strength is [strength]. When facing [challenge], I [action] and achieved [verified result]. This taught me [transferable skill], which I could apply to [specific job contribution].”

## 5. Why corporate R&D?
“I am interested in [company research theme] because [specific reason]. My experience with [research method and evidence] could help me contribute to [actual team responsibility].” Do not submit before verifying the company's work.

## 6. Why AI / scientific computing?
“I want to translate scientific computing from a research tool into software and technical platforms that solve real problems. Through [verified numerical modeling or programming work], I learned [skill]. I am interested in contributing to [company product / actual role].”

## 7. Why consulting?
“I enjoy structuring ambiguous problems, building hypotheses, and testing them with evidence. I would like to explore applying [research-based analytical and communication skills] to [specific client problems].” Interest in consulting remains a hypothesis.

## 8. Interview question bank
Why a PhD? What is original and reproducible in your research? How do simulations connect to experiments? Describe failed calculations and corrections. Explain a team disagreement. Why industry rather than academia? Why this role and employer? Why Tokyo? How do you balance compensation, learning, and work content? Describe Japanese and English communication experience.

## 9. Questions for employers
- How are doctoral hires placed into initial research teams?
- How do computational and experimental groups collaborate?
- How is R&D performance evaluated?
- How do worksite, remote-work, and transfer policies differ by role?
- Where can I confirm doctoral pay, housing benefits, and bonus eligibility?

## 10. Four-week preparation plan
- Week 1: Answer ten values prompts; write three evidence-based research stories.
- Week 2: Draft 30-second, two-minute, and five-minute research pitches in both languages.
- Week 3: Draft role-specific motivations for one R&D, one AI, and one consulting company.
- Week 4: Mock interview, questions for recruiters, and a benefits comparison.

**Before submitting:** Verify every claim, metric, employer name, hiring year, and eligibility condition.
