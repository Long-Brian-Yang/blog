# Class of 2029: Parallel Career Exploration Strategy

Updated 2026-10-27. User preference: **explore all five paths**—fundamental corporate research, applied industrial R&D, AI/scientific computing, consulting/technology strategy, and generalist/cross-functional roles. No ranking or industry commitment yet.

## Shared positioning
- Translate a computational materials PhD background into role-specific evidence.
- Prioritize actual Tokyo worksite, pay, housing, holidays, and working conditions.
- Compare daily work, hiring requirements, and real interview feedback—not just perceived prestige.
- Never exaggerate research achievements, coding proficiency, or language skills.

## Five parallel tracks
| Track | Transferable evidence | Key unknowns | Deliverable |
|---|---|---|---|
| A Fundamental research | DFT/AIMD, hypotheses, validation | Lab topic, doctoral hiring | Research summary and technical interview |
| B Applied R&D | Materials physics, catalysis/energy, modeling | Experimental and product connection | Application-focused motivation |
| C AI / scientific computing | Numerical methods, ML potentials, code | Algorithms, engineering readiness | Technical portfolio |
| D Consulting / tech strategy | Problem framing, hypothesis testing, communication | Case interviews, business knowledge | STAR stories and case practice |
| E Generalist / cross-functional | Learning, collaboration, communication | Enjoyment of day-to-day work, eligibility | General-purpose personal statement |

## Decision method
After reviewing actual jobs, personally score each track 1–5 on interest, fit, preparation burden, Tokyo worksite certainty, compensation/benefits, and future options. Do not prefill personal preferences.

## Preparation order
1. Shared evidence: 3–5 stories on research, outcomes, setbacks, and teamwork.
2. Shared materials: Japanese resume, English CV, 30-second and 90-second introduction.
3. Role-specific: A/B research summary; C technical portfolio; D case practice; E generalist personal statement.
4. Compare 2–3 actual openings per track and their requirements and benefits.
5. Reassess after information sessions, informal chats, internships, and interview feedback.
