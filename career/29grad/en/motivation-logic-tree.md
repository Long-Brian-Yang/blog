# Class of 2029: Five-Track Motivation Logic Tree

Updated 2026-10-27. All motivations below are **candidate framings**, not confirmed personal preferences.

## Core thread
Past: computational materials PhD training -> present: first-principles methods, dynamics and machine learning -> possible future: apply modeling, numerical methods and structured problem-solving to broader practical challenges (**requires user confirmation**).

## Why industry instead of a postdoc or faculty?
Do not disparage academia or disguise Tokyo/pay preferences as scientific motivation.
Candidate reasons to confirm: applied impact, interdisciplinary teams, research-to-product connection, career development, long-term life plans.
Structure: respect academia -> authentic preferred working style and goal -> specific industry role fit -> evidence.

## Why explore five tracks?
Shared transferable capabilities do not mean identical jobs. Keep distinct job-specific reasons. In an application, do not say “I would take any role.”

## Track-specific hypotheses
**A Fundamental R&D:** scientific questions, mechanisms, validation and sustained research. Verify research group, doctoral roles, publication policies.
**B Applied materials/energy R&D:** proton-conducting materials and atomistic insight; experimental collaboration is a potential interest, not an invented accomplishment. Verify products and cross-functional work.
**C AI/scientific computing:** ML interatomic potentials, dataset quality, numerical stability and downstream evaluation. Verify actual coding, training and engineering experience.
**D Consulting/tech strategy:** structuring complex issues, evaluating evidence, communicating uncertainty. Verify behavioral examples, business literacy and case expectations.
**E Generalist:** learning, collaboration and project execution. Verify rotation, duties and reasons for non-research roles.

## Why company formula
Industry opportunity (externally verified) + company-specific difference (official source) + personal evidence (evidence bank) + desired contribution (job posting) + a thoughtful reverse question.
Avoid generic claims about prestige, innovation or globalization.

## Keep job fit and conditions separate
Tokyo actual worksite (not HQ alone); PhD compensation, bonus, housing, vacation, flexibility; research autonomy, team, growth and impact. Decide when to discuss conditions based on the interview stage.

## HR consistency test
Why industry? Why this role? Why this employer? Why you? What can you contribute in 3 years? Where do you hope to develop in 5 years? Every answer needs authentic motivation, a role-specific task and verified evidence.

## Candidate universal value statement
“I am interested in modeling complex problems, validating results, and making decisions grounded in evidence. I hope to apply the mindset developed during my doctorate to specific challenges in this role.”
Tailor to each job and verify authenticity.

## Questions still to confirm
Top 2–3 reasons for industry; attractive tasks in each track; unacceptable working conditions; skills to build in the first two years.
