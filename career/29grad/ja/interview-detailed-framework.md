Verified facts and evidence -> personal competencies, stories, research pitch and values -> role/company-specific fit -> ES, CV, interviews, questions and debriefs.

## Deliverable specification
- Identity: 1-sentence professional positioning, 30-second and 60-second introductions, plus a 2-minute version.
- Evidence: 5-8 stories, each with challenge, personal decisions/actions, measurable or observable outcome, lessons, unresolved questions, and proof links.
- ACS Omega: 400-character Japanese ES draft, 60-second and 3-minute oral versions, and a 15-minute technical defense; never claim acceptance.
- Research: layperson 1-minute pitch, specialist 5-minute overview, 15-minute discussion, 1-2 page research summary and optional slides.
- Technical defense: DFT, AIMD, NEP/MACE/SevenNet, fine-tuning, train/test leakage, force RMSE, NVE drift versus fluctuations, MSD statistics, RDF, diffusion coefficients, experimental comparisons and limitations.
- Motivation: why industry; five separate why-role narratives; company-specific why-company/why-team grounded in public job and R&D information.
- Behavioral: failure, conflict, collaboration, leadership, initiative, ethical judgment, ambiguity, adaptation, time management, communicating to nonexperts.
- Technical/case: role-dependent coding, numerical reasoning, scientific model validation, business case and structured hypothesis testing.
- Conditions: Tokyo workplace, doctoral pay, housing, holidays, flexibility, work authorization and language requirements; verify per posting, not as unsupported assumptions.
- Language: Japanese and English 30s/1m/3m answers, terminology glossary, follow-up question drills.
- Mock interviews: record question, answer duration, clarity, evidence, personal ownership, technical accuracy, fit and follow-up improvement.

## Story-to-question mapping
| Source story | Primary questions | Watchouts |
|---|---|---|
| ACS Omega multi-round review | major effort, resilience, validation, feedback | Ongoing, not accepted; clarify own actions |
| Independent research decision | initiative, creativity, technical trade-offs | Actual example not yet verified |
| Failed calculation or unexpected finding | failure, diagnosis, learning | No fabricated result |
| Collaboration or disagreement | teamwork, communication, leadership | Specify roles and actual resolution |
| Learning a new method | learning agility, career transition | Specify starting point and evidence |

## Evaluation rubric (0-2 each)
- Clear conclusion and relevance to question.
- Specific personal action and judgment.
- Factual evidence and defensible result.
- Reflection and awareness of limitations.
- Transfer to target role.
Score 0=missing, 1=partial, 2=convincing. Review each answer for contradiction with other answers.

## Interview depth ladder
- 30 seconds: headline and value.
- 1 minute: problem, action, outcome.
- 3 minutes: challenge, trade-off, evidence, lesson.
- 5-15 minutes: methodological details, alternatives, limits and follow-up questions.

## Four-week implementation
Week 1: evidence and personal ownership; week 2: bilingual core answers and research pitch; week 3: five career-track adaptations and technical/case work; week 4: mock interviews, company-specific fit and revisions.
