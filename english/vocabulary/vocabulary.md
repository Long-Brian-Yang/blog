# Vocabulary & Chunks

This file collects **reusable expressions**, not just isolated vocabulary.

## Work, Research & Industry

| Expression | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| prepare someone for ~ | 让某人为……做好准备 | A PhD prepares you for solving open-ended problems. |
| have immediate practical value | 有即时实际价值 | The result may not have immediate practical value. |
| good enough | 足够好 | Sometimes a good-enough solution is all you need. |
| deliver something on time | 按时交付 | We need to deliver the results on time. |
| care more about whether... | 更在意是否…… | Companies may care more about whether the method actually works. |
| reliable and scalable | 可靠且可扩展 | The method needs to be reliable and scalable. |
| deal with uncertainty | 处理不确定性 | Research teaches you how to deal with uncertainty. |
| a clear roadmap | 清晰的路线图 | Nobody gives you a clear roadmap in research. |
| come up with a solution | 想出解决方案 | We need to come up with another solution. |
| balance A with B | 平衡 A 与 B | We need to balance accuracy with computational cost. |
| practical constraints | 现实限制 | Industrial research often involves practical constraints. |
| figure things out | 自己弄明白 / 找到办法 | You often have to figure things out yourself. |

## Review rule

When reviewing an expression:

1. Read the English chunk.
2. Hide the example.
3. Make a new sentence from your own life or research.
4. Say it aloud.
