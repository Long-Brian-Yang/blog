# 2026-10-07 — How to Disagree Without Sounding Rude

> **Focus:** disagreement · discussion skills · workplace & academic English  
> **Goal:** disagree clearly and naturally without sounding aggressive, overly apologetic, or vague.

---

## 1. Reading

Disagreeing with someone in English can be surprisingly difficult.

The problem is not usually knowing how to say that you disagree. Expressions like *I disagree* or *I don't think so* are simple enough. The harder part is expressing a different opinion without making the conversation feel confrontational.

In many situations, especially at work or in academic discussions, disagreement is not something you need to avoid. In fact, being able to challenge an idea constructively is an important communication skill. The key is to make it clear that you are questioning the idea rather than attacking the person.

One useful strategy is to acknowledge the other person's point before introducing your own. You might say, "I see where you're coming from, but I'm not sure I completely agree." This shows that you have understood their reasoning, even if you reach a different conclusion.

Another approach is to frame your disagreement as a concern or a question. Instead of saying, "That won't work," you could say, "My concern is that this might not work well when the system gets larger." The underlying message is similar, but the second version gives the other person something specific to respond to.

It is also important not to soften your language so much that your actual opinion disappears. If you strongly believe that a conclusion is not supported by the evidence, you should be able to say so clearly. Being polite does not mean avoiding disagreement altogether.

Ultimately, good disagreement is about balancing clarity with respect. A productive discussion is not one in which everyone agrees. It is one in which people can disagree without turning the disagreement into a personal conflict.

## 2. 中文理解

英语里真正困难的并不是说出“我不同意”，而是**如何清楚表达不同意见，同时又不让交流变得具有对抗性**。

特别是在工作和学术讨论中，disagreement 并不是需要避免的事情。能够建设性地质疑一个观点，本身就是重要的沟通能力。核心原则是：

> **Challenge the idea, not the person.**  
> 质疑观点，而不是攻击提出观点的人。

一个很好用的方法是先表明自己理解对方为什么这么想：

> I see where you're coming from.

然后再提出自己的保留意见：

> I'm not sure I completely agree.

还可以把“反对”转化成一个具体的 **concern**：

> My concern is that we don't have enough data yet.

这样讨论的重点就从“谁对谁错”变成了“这里具体存在什么问题”。

不过，礼貌也不意味着没有立场。如果证据确实不足，就应该明确地说：

> I don't think the evidence supports that conclusion.

真正成熟的 disagreement，就是在 **clarity** 和 **respect** 之间找到平衡。

### Usage Notes

- **evidence** 通常是不可数名词：*some evidence / more evidence / sufficient evidence / a piece of evidence*，而不是 *an evidence / many evidences*。
- **causal ≠ casual**: *causal* = 因果的；*casual* = 随意的。
- **acknowledge ≠ agree**: *acknowledge someone's point* 是承认/回应对方观点，不代表同意最终结论。

## 4. Key Chunks & Grammar

| Expression | 中文 | Usage |
|---|---|---|
| **I see where you're coming from.** | 我理解你为什么这么想 | 先承认对方逻辑 |
| **That's a fair point.** | 这个观点有道理 | 接受部分合理性 |
| **I'm not sure I completely agree.** | 我不确定自己完全同意 | 委婉反对 |
| **I would look at it differently.** | 我会从不同角度看 | 引出自己的观点 |
| **The way I see it,...** | 在我看来…… | 自然表达立场 |
| **My concern is that...** | 我的顾虑是…… | 工作/研究高频 |
| **I'm not convinced that...** | 我还没有被说服…… | 比较明确的保留意见 |
| **I would argue that...** | 我的观点是…… | 学术讨论 |
| **That said,...** | 话虽如此…… | 让步后转折 |
| **draw a conclusion** | 得出结论 | 高频搭配 |
| **raise a concern** | 提出顾虑 | meeting 高频 |
| **establish a causal relationship** | 建立因果关系 | 科研高频 |

### Grammar Patterns

**I'm not sure + clause**

> I'm not sure this is the best interpretation.

这里的 *I'm not sure* 有时是一种礼貌的 disagreement。

**My concern is that...**

> My concern is that this approach may not generalize well to larger systems.

把对人的否定变成对具体问题的讨论。

**I would argue that...**

> I would argue that the main limitation is the dataset size.

Academic English 中的 *argue* 更接近“提出论点”，并不是“吵架”。

**That's a fair point. That said,...**

> That's a fair point. That said, I think we still need more data.

**I'm not saying X. I'm just saying Y.**

> I'm not saying the model is bad. I'm just saying we need more evidence before concluding that it's better.

### Disagreement Scale

| 强度 | Expression |
|---|---|
| 🟢 很轻 | I see what you mean. |
| 🟢 轻 | That's a fair point. |
| 🟡 保留意见 | I'm not sure I completely agree. |
| 🟡 明确立场 | I would look at it differently. |
| 🟠 较强 | I'm not convinced that the evidence supports that. |
| 🔴 明确反对 | I don't think that conclusion is supported by the evidence. |

## 5. Natural Spoken English

> I see what you mean, / but I'm not sure I completely agree.
>
> I think the main issue is that / we're looking at the average performance, / but that doesn't necessarily tell us / how reliable the model is for individual structures.
>
> So I would probably look at it slightly differently.
>
> I'm not saying the model isn't good. / My concern is just that / we might be drawing a stronger conclusion / than the data actually supports.
>
> I think we'd need a little more evidence / before we can confidently say / that one model is better than the other.

### Speaking Tip

表达 disagreement 时不用刻意说得很快。特别是 *I'm not sure / I completely agree* 和 *My concern is that / ...*，稍微停顿反而更自然、更有分寸。

## 6. Expression Upgrade

**我不同意**

> Basic: I disagree.  
> **Better: I'm not sure I completely agree with that.**

**我觉得你的解释不太对**

> Basic: I think your explanation is wrong.  
> **Better: I'm not sure I'd interpret the results that way.**

**数据还不够**

> Basic: We don't have enough data.  
> **Better: I'm not convinced we have enough evidence to support that conclusion yet.**

**这个方法可能有问题**

> **My concern is that this approach may not generalize well to larger systems.**

**你说得有道理，但是……**

> **That's a fair point. That said, I think there's another issue we need to consider.**

**我不是说这个模型不好**

> **I'm not saying the model is bad. I'm just not convinced that it's consistently better.**

### Research Examples

**Model comparison**

> MACE is clearly better than the other models.

> **I'm not sure I'd go that far. The average error is lower, but I'm not convinced that the difference is large enough to conclude that MACE is consistently better.**

**Diffusion**

> The higher diffusion coefficient proves that proton transport is faster.

> **That's a fair point, but I think we also need to look at the statistical uncertainty before drawing that conclusion.**

**Mechanism**

> The faster diffusion is caused by stronger Y–H attraction.

> **That's certainly one possible explanation, but I'm not sure we have enough evidence to establish a causal relationship yet.**

## 8. Speaking & Review

### Mini Conversation

**Colleague**

> I think we should just use the model with the lowest RMSE.

**You**

> I see where you're coming from, but I'm not sure RMSE alone is enough.

**Colleague**

> Why not?

**You**

> My concern is that a low average error doesn't necessarily mean the model is reliable for the configurations we actually care about.

**Colleague**

> So what would you suggest?

**You**

> I'd probably look at the force errors, outliers, and MD stability as well before making the final decision.

### Free Speaking

回答 **2 分钟**：

> **Do you find it easy to disagree with people directly?**

可以谈：

- whether you tend to avoid conflict
- friends vs. professors vs. colleagues
- direct vs. indirect communication
- disagreement in research
- when being too polite becomes a problem

尽量主动使用：

*I see where you're coming from · that's a fair point · I'm not sure I completely agree · my concern is that · I would argue that · I'm not convinced that · draw a conclusion*

### One-Minute Version

> I don't think disagreement is necessarily a bad thing, especially in research.
>
> If everyone agrees all the time, you might miss important problems. But I also think the way you disagree matters.
>
> Instead of just saying, "You're wrong," I prefer to explain what specifically concerns me. I might say, "I see where you're coming from, but I'm not sure the evidence supports that conclusion."
>
> That way, you're still being clear about your opinion, but you're keeping the discussion focused on the idea rather than making it personal.

### Today's Core Pattern

**Acknowledge → Disagree → Explain → Suggest**

> **I see what you mean.**  
> **I'm not sure I completely agree, though.**  
> **My concern is that we haven't tested the model on enough structures.**  
> **I'd probably run another validation set before drawing that conclusion.**
