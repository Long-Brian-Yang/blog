# 2026-10-05 — Why Is It So Hard to Slow Down?

> **Focus:** daily-life discussion · emotions & productivity · natural spoken English  
> **Goal:** learn to express abstract personal feelings naturally and talk about productivity without relying on simple words like *busy*, *tired*, or *stressed*.

---

## 1. Reading

I sometimes find it surprisingly difficult to slow down, even when I don't have anything urgent to do.

When you've been busy for a long time, being productive can almost become a habit. You get used to having something on your schedule, checking messages, thinking about unfinished work, or planning what to do next. Then, when you finally have some free time, you don't necessarily feel relaxed. Instead, you might start wondering whether you're wasting time.

I think part of the problem is that productivity is easy to measure. You can count how many tasks you've completed, how many papers you've read, or how many hours you've worked. Rest is different. There is usually nothing concrete to show for it, so it can feel less valuable even though you know intellectually that you need it.

There's also a strange feeling that if you stop moving forward, you might fall behind. This is especially true when you're surrounded by ambitious people. Someone is always publishing another paper, learning another skill, getting another job offer, or achieving something that makes you question whether you're doing enough.

But constantly trying to be productive has its own cost. If every free hour becomes an opportunity to work, study, or improve yourself, then rest starts to feel like another task that needs to be optimized.

I'm gradually realizing that doing nothing for a while isn't necessarily a waste of time. Sometimes you need space where nothing is expected of you. You don't need to turn every experience into progress.

Maybe slowing down isn't the opposite of moving forward. Sometimes, it's part of it.

## 2. 中文理解

我有时候会发现，即使没有什么紧急的事情，真正让自己慢下来居然也很困难。

当一个人长期处于忙碌状态时，“保持高效率”本身几乎会变成一种习惯。你习惯了日程表上一直有事情，习惯查看消息、想着还没完成的工作，或者计划下一步要做什么。于是，当你终于有了一些空闲时间，你反而不一定会感到放松，而可能开始怀疑自己是不是在浪费时间。

我觉得其中一个原因是，**生产力很容易被量化，而休息很难。**你可以计算完成了多少任务、读了多少论文、工作了多少小时。但是休息通常不会留下什么具体的成果，所以即使理性上知道自己需要休息，还是可能觉得它“不够有价值”。

还有一种很奇怪的感觉：**如果自己不继续向前，就可能会落后。**尤其当周围都是很有上进心的人时，这种感觉会更明显。总有人又发表了一篇论文、学会了一个新技能、拿到了新的 offer，或者取得了某种成就，让你开始怀疑自己是不是做得还不够。

但一直追求 productive 本身也是有代价的。如果每一个空闲小时都变成了工作、学习或者“提升自己”的机会，那么最后连休息都会变成另一个需要被“优化”的任务。

我正在慢慢意识到，偶尔什么都不做并不一定是在浪费时间。有时候，人需要一段**没有任何事情被期待**的空间。并不是每一段经历都必须转化成所谓的“成长”。

也许，慢下来并不是前进的反面。

**有时候，它本身就是前进的一部分。**

## 3. Key Vocabulary & Chunks

不要只记 `busy / tired / relax`。今天重点学习如何描述更复杂的心理状态。

| Expression | 中文 | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **slow down** | 慢下来、放慢节奏 | I find it difficult to slow down after a busy week. |
| **have something urgent to do** | 有紧急的事情要做 | I don't have anything urgent to do today. |
| **become a habit** | 变成一种习惯 | Checking my phone first thing in the morning became a habit. |
| **get used to doing something** | 习惯做某事 | I got used to working under pressure. |
| **unfinished work** | 尚未完成的工作 | I keep thinking about unfinished work. |
| **waste time** | 浪费时间 | Sometimes I feel like I'm wasting time when I'm resting. |
| **easy to measure** | 容易衡量 | Productivity is relatively easy to measure. |
| **have nothing concrete to show for it** | 没有什么具体成果可以展示 | I studied all day but had nothing concrete to show for it. |
| **fall behind** | 落后、掉队 | I'm worried that I'll fall behind if I stop studying. |
| **be surrounded by...** | 被……包围 / 身边都是…… | I'm surrounded by highly motivated people. |
| **question whether...** | 开始怀疑是否…… | It made me question whether I was doing enough. |
| **have its own cost** | 本身也有代价 | Constant productivity has its own cost. |
| **turn A into B** | 把 A 变成 B | Don't turn every hobby into another competition. |
| **nothing is expected of you** | 没有人要求你做什么 | Everyone needs time when nothing is expected of them. |
| **not necessarily** | 未必、不一定 | Rest isn't necessarily a waste of time. |
| **move forward** | 向前走、取得进展 | I still want to keep moving forward. |

### 特别值得记的三个

#### have nothing concrete to show for it

非常自然。

> I've been working all morning, but I have nothing concrete to show for it.

不是说“什么都没做”，而是：**做了很多，但是没有一个明确可展示的成果。**

#### fall behind

比：

> Other people are becoming better than me.

自然太多。

> Sometimes I feel like I'll fall behind if I take a break.

#### question whether I'm doing enough

非常适合表达这种心理：

> Seeing other people's achievements sometimes makes me question whether I'm doing enough.

不是简单：

> I feel stressed.

而是准确描述**压力是怎么产生的**。

## 4. Grammar & Usage

### 4.1 find it + adjective + to do

> I **find it difficult to slow down**.

> I **find it hard to completely disconnect from work**.

结构：

> find + it + adjective + to do something

### 4.2 get used to + -ing

> You **get used to having something on your schedule**.

> I **got used to working under pressure**.

注意：

> get used to **working**

而不是：

> ❌ get used to work

### 4.3 even though + clause

> Rest can feel less valuable **even though you know intellectually that you need it**.

> I still feel guilty **even though I know I need a break**.

### 4.4 make someone question whether...

> Seeing other people's achievements **makes me question whether I'm doing enough**.

> It made me question whether I had made the right decision.

### 4.5 If A, then B

> **If every free hour becomes an opportunity to work, then rest starts to feel like another task.**

> If everything becomes a competition, then it's difficult to enjoy the process.

### 4.6 isn't necessarily...

> Doing nothing **isn't necessarily a waste of time**.

它比绝对表达更加克制，也更适合复杂观点。

## 5. Natural Spoken English

> I don't know why, but sometimes I actually find it really hard to do nothing.
>
> Like, when I've been busy for a long time, I get so used to always having something to do that free time starts to feel kind of uncomfortable.
>
> Even when I finally have a day off, part of me is still thinking, "Okay, what should I be doing right now?"
>
> And I think social comparison makes it worse. You see someone publishing another paper or getting a great job, and suddenly you're like, "Wait, am I doing enough?"
>
> But recently I've been trying to remind myself that not everything has to be productive. Sometimes it's okay to just have a completely useless afternoon.
>
> I don't need to optimize every hour of my life.

## 6. Pronunciation & Thought Groups

不要逐词：

> I / DON'T / KNOW / WHY / BUT / SOMETIMES...

尝试：

> I don't know why, /  
> but sometimes /  
> I actually find it really hard /  
> to do nothing.

再比如：

> I get so used to /  
> always having something to do /  
> that free time /  
> starts to feel kind of uncomfortable.

以及：

> I don't need to optimize /  
> every hour of my life.

### 今天特别注意

`kind of` 在自然口语中通常不会每个音都非常清楚：

> kind of uncomfortable

## 7. Expression Upgrade

### ① 我最近很忙

**Basic**

> I've been very busy recently.

**Better**

> **I've had a lot going on lately.**

或者：

> **My schedule has been pretty packed lately.**

### ② 我觉得别人都比我厉害

**Basic**

> I feel like everyone is better than me.

**Better**

> **Seeing what everyone else is doing sometimes makes me feel like I'm falling behind.**

### ③ 我休息的时候也觉得有压力

**Basic**

> I feel stressed even when I rest.

**Better**

> **Even when I have time off, I find it hard to completely switch off.**

这里的 **switch off** 表示 mentally stop thinking about work。

### ④ 我总觉得应该做点什么

**Basic**

> I always think I should do something.

**Better**

> **I always have this feeling that I should be doing something productive.**

### ⑤ 我想好好休息

**Basic**

> I want to have a good rest.

**More natural**

> **I just want to take some time off.**

或者：

> **I need some time to properly unwind.**

## 8. Active Output Practice

先不要看答案。

1. 最近事情很多，我已经习惯一直保持忙碌的状态。
2. 即使有空，我有时候也很难完全放松下来。
3. 看到别人取得很多成果的时候，我偶尔会觉得自己是不是落后了。
4. 我正在慢慢意识到，不是所有时间都必须是“有生产力的”。
5. 什么都不做并不一定是在浪费时间。
6. 我不需要优化自己人生里的每一个小时。
7. 有时候，人需要一段完全没有任何事情被期待的时间。

### Suggested Answers

1. **I've had a lot going on lately, so I've gotten used to always being busy.**
2. **Even when I have some free time, I sometimes find it hard to completely switch off.**
3. **When I see other people achieving a lot, I sometimes feel like I'm falling behind.**
4. **I'm gradually realizing that not every moment has to be productive.**
5. **Doing nothing isn't necessarily a waste of time.**
6. **I don't need to optimize every hour of my life.**
7. **Sometimes you need time when nothing is expected of you.**

更高级的第 3 句：

> Seeing other people's achievements sometimes makes me question whether I'm doing enough.

## 9. Free Speaking Challenge

回答：

> **Do you find it difficult to relax without feeling guilty about it?**

可以讲：

- what happens when you finally have free time
- whether you compare yourself with other people
- whether research makes this worse
- what you usually do to unwind
- whether your attitude toward rest has changed

目标 **2 分钟**。

尽量主动使用：

- have a lot going on
- switch off
- fall behind
- question whether I'm doing enough
- not necessarily
- unwind
- nothing is expected of me

---

## 10. One-Minute Version

> I think I've gotten so used to being busy that sometimes I actually find it difficult to relax.
>
> Even when I have free time, part of me feels like I should be doing something productive. And when I see other people achieving things, it's easy to feel like I'm falling behind.
>
> But recently I've been trying to change that mindset. I'm realizing that rest isn't necessarily a waste of time, and I don't need to optimize every hour of my life.
>
> Sometimes doing nothing is exactly what I need.
