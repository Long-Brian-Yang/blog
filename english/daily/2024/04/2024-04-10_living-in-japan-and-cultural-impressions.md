# 2024-04-10 — Living in Japan: Expectations, Culture & Social Values

> **Focus:** discussion English · cultural comparison · expressing nuanced opinions

## 1. Reading

Before living in Japan, it is easy to form an image of the country through anime, films, travel videos, or short visits. Daily life, however, often gives you a more complicated picture.

One thing that stands out is the importance of social etiquette. People tend to pay attention to how their behavior affects others, whether they are using public transportation, speaking in a shared space, or interacting with colleagues. This can make everyday life feel orderly and comfortable, but it can also create pressure to understand rules that are not always explained directly.

Another interesting aspect is the balance between tradition and modern life. Traditional festivals, local customs, temples, and seasonal events remain visible, while cities are highly modern and technologically advanced. These two sides do not necessarily contradict each other; they often coexist naturally.

Living in another country also changes the way you think about your own habits and values. Instead of simply deciding that one culture is better than another, it becomes more useful to ask why certain behaviors make sense in a particular social context.

## 2. 中文理解

在真正长期生活之前，人们容易通过动漫、电影、旅行视频或短期旅行形成对日本的印象。但日常生活通常会呈现更复杂的一面。

日本社会比较强调礼仪以及自己的行为对他人的影响。这会让公共生活显得有秩序，但也可能让外来者需要花时间理解很多没有被明确说出来的规则。

另一个值得讨论的地方是传统与现代生活的共存。与其简单判断哪个文化“更好”，更成熟的表达方式是理解某种行为为什么会在特定社会背景下形成。

## 3. Key Vocabulary & Chunks

| Expression | 中文 | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **form an image of...** | 形成对……的印象 | People often form an image of Japan through media. |
| **daily life gives you a different picture** | 日常生活呈现不同的一面 | Living there gives you a different picture. |
| **stand out** | 特别明显 / 引人注意 | One thing that stands out is the etiquette. |
| **pay attention to how...** | 注意……如何…… | People pay attention to how their behavior affects others. |
| **social etiquette** | 社会礼仪 | Social etiquette plays an important role. |
| **create pressure to...** | 产生……的压力 | It can create pressure to follow unspoken rules. |
| **unspoken rules** | 不成文规则 | Some social rules are unspoken. |
| **the balance between A and B** | A 与 B 之间的平衡 | the balance between tradition and modern life |
| **coexist naturally** | 自然共存 | The two sides often coexist naturally. |
| **social context** | 社会背景 | The behavior makes sense in that social context. |

## 4. Grammar & Usage

### It is easy to + verb

> It is easy to form an image of a country through media.

适合引入“表面印象 vs 实际体验”。

### whether A, B, or C

> ...whether they are using public transportation, speaking in a shared space, or interacting with colleagues.

用于自然列举多个情境。

### Instead of + -ing

> Instead of simply deciding that one culture is better, it is more useful to ask why.

### not necessarily

> The two sides do not necessarily contradict each other.

“不一定”比绝对否定更适合复杂观点。

## 5. Natural Spoken English

Before I actually lived in Japan, I think my image of the country was much simpler. Once you live here, you start noticing all these small social rules and expectations.

I really like how considerate people can be in public spaces, but at the same time, it sometimes takes a while to understand what you're expected to do because nobody explains every rule directly.

I also find the mix of traditional culture and modern life really interesting. They don't feel mutually exclusive. They just coexist.

## 6. Expression Upgrade

**Basic**

> Japan is very polite.

**Better**

> People tend to be very conscious of how their behavior affects others.

**Basic**

> Japan has old culture and new technology.

**Better**

> Traditional culture and highly modern urban life coexist surprisingly naturally.
