# 2024-11-28 — Research Email: Asking About an iPad

> **Focus:** formal research email · polite requests · explaining research needs

## Model English

I would like to seek your advice about whether it would be possible to apply for an iPad to support my research work.

I have completed most of my coursework and am now focusing more on research. I believe an iPad would be particularly useful for organizing notes, reading papers, and annotating documents.

I would appreciate your advice on whether there is an appropriate procedure for making such a request.

## 中文理解

核心是礼貌说明研究设备需求：课程基本完成、研究比重增加，设备主要用于整理笔记、阅读论文和批注文档，并询问合适的申请流程。

## Vocabulary & Chunks

| Expression | 中文 |
|---|---|
| seek your advice | 征求您的建议 |
| support my research work | 辅助研究工作 |
| complete my coursework | 完成课程学习 |
| focus more on research | 把更多精力放在研究上 |
| organize notes | 整理笔记 |
| read papers | 阅读论文 |
| annotate documents | 批注文档 |
| appropriate procedure | 合适的流程 |

## Grammar & Usage

**would like to + verb** 比 *I want to* 更适合正式邮件。

> I would like to seek your advice.

**whether it would be possible to...** 可以柔和地询问可能性。

**be useful for + -ing**

> It would be useful for reading papers and annotating documents.

## Natural Spoken English

I'm spending more of my time on research now, so I've been thinking about getting an iPad for things like reading papers, taking notes, and annotating documents.

## Active Output

1. 我想请教一下是否有相应的申请流程。
2. 我现在把更多精力放在研究上。
3. 这个设备主要会用于阅读论文和整理笔记。

### Suggested Answers

1. I'd like to ask whether there is an appropriate procedure for making this request.
2. I'm focusing more on research now.
3. I would mainly use the device for reading papers and organizing notes.

## Retelling

Explain in 30–60 seconds why a tablet could be useful for research.

## Review

Reuse: **seek your advice · focus on · be useful for · appropriate procedure**.
