# 2024-10-08 — Academic Self-Introduction: SOFC Research

> **Focus:** first academic introduction · polite research English · introducing affiliation and topic

## 1. Reading

Hello, my name is Long Yang. I am conducting research in Professor Ishikawa's laboratory at Tokyo Institute of Technology.

My research is related to solid oxide fuel cells, or SOFCs. I am interested in understanding materials and transport phenomena through computational approaches.

I am very glad to have the opportunity to introduce myself and my research. I look forward to learning from everyone and working together.

## 2. 中文理解

这是典型的学术场合初次自我介绍：先说明姓名和所属研究室，再用一两句话概括研究主题，最后用礼貌但不过度正式的方式表达期待交流与合作。

## 3. Key Vocabulary & Chunks

**conduct research in...** — 在……开展研究  
**be related to...** — 与……相关  
**solid oxide fuel cells (SOFCs)** — 固体氧化物燃料电池  
**computational approaches** — 计算方法  
**have the opportunity to...** — 有机会……  
**look forward to learning from...** — 期待向……学习  
**work together** — 一起合作

## 4. Grammar & Usage

### My research is related to...

适合刚开始介绍研究领域，但如果要更有力，可以升级成：

> My research focuses on...

### look forward to + -ing

> I look forward to **learning** from everyone and **working** together.

### I am conducting research...

强调目前正在进行的研究工作。

## 5. Natural Spoken English

Hi, I'm Long Yang. I'm working in Professor Ishikawa's group at Tokyo Tech. My research is mainly related to solid oxide fuel cells, and I use computational methods to study materials and transport behavior. It's great to meet everyone, and I'm looking forward to learning from you and working together.

## 6. Expression Upgrade

**Basic**

> My research is about SOFC.

**Better**

> My research focuses on computational studies of materials and transport phenomena in solid oxide fuel cells.

**Basic**

> Nice to meet you. Please take care of me.

**Natural English**

> It's great to meet everyone. I'm looking forward to learning from you and working together.
