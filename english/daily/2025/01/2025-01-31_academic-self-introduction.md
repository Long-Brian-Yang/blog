# 2025-01-31 — Academic Self-Introduction: From Data Science to Materials Science

> **Focus:** academic self-introduction · research journey · self-directed learning

## Reading

My academic background started in data science. During my undergraduate studies, I became interested in deep learning through a research project on CT medical-image segmentation.

Because much of the work took place during the COVID period, I had to learn independently. I studied deep learning through online courses, CS231n, PyTorch resources, open-source projects, and research papers. That experience taught me how to learn new technical topics on my own and solve problems without clear step-by-step instructions.

Later, I moved toward the intersection of machine learning and materials science. My research focuses on using neural-network potentials to accelerate molecular dynamics simulations and study diffusion and material properties.

Compared with my undergraduate experience, graduate research has also taught me the importance of collaboration, regular discussion, and adapting my research plan as new results emerge.

## 中文理解

主线是：**Data Science → Deep Learning → Independent Learning → ML + Materials Science → Research Collaboration**。重点不是机械罗列学历，而是说明能力如何一步步发展。
