# 2025-01-22 — CV English: Concise Research & Project Bullets

> **Focus:** concise professional English · action verbs · CV phrasing

## Core Examples

- **Developing and fine-tuning NNPs and co-authoring reports**
- **Integrated APIs and led database design in a cross-cultural team.**
- **English (Fluent, TOEFL 90)**

## 中文理解

CV 英语重点不是写长句，而是用明确的动作动词快速说明“做了什么 + 贡献是什么”。

## Vocabulary & Chunks

**develop and fine-tune** — 开发并微调  
**co-author reports** — 共同撰写报告  
**integrate APIs** — 整合 API  
**lead database design** — 主导数据库设计  
**cross-cultural team** — 跨文化团队

## Grammar & Usage

并列动作保持平行结构：

> Developing **and fine-tuning** NNPs

CV 优先使用具体 action verbs：

> developed · integrated · led · designed · analyzed · implemented

少用模糊的 *did / worked on / helped with*。

## Natural Spoken English

In my research, I develop and fine-tune neural-network potentials and contribute to research reports. I've also worked in cross-cultural teams, where I integrated APIs and took responsibility for database design.

## Active Output

1. 开发并微调神经网络势。
2. 在跨文化团队中负责数据库设计。
3. 参与研究报告撰写。

### Suggested Answers

1. Developed and fine-tuned neural-network potentials.
2. Led database design in a cross-cultural team.
3. Co-authored research reports.

Give a 30-second summary of your technical experience using at least three strong action verbs.
