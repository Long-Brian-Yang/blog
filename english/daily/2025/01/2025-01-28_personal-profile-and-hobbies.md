# 2025-01-28 — Interview English: Personal Profile, Hobbies & Life Outside Research

> **Focus:** concise personal introduction · conversational interview English · hobbies

## 1. Reading

Besides research, I enjoy working on technical projects and maintaining my GitHub and academic pages. I also value opportunities to learn with other people through study groups and research activities.

Outside academics, I like basketball and skiing. I started skiing in college, and it became one of the activities I really enjoy. During my master's studies, I also try to stay active by going to the gym and walking regularly.

I think these activities help me maintain a good balance between research and daily life.

## 2. 中文理解

面试中的个人介绍不需要把兴趣爱好讲得很复杂。重点是简洁地展示“研究之外的自己”，让内容有一点人格感，同时保持专业。

## 3. Key Vocabulary & Chunks

**besides research** — 除了研究之外  
**technical projects** — 技术项目  
**maintain my GitHub / academic page** — 维护 GitHub / 学术主页  
**study groups** — 学习小组  
**outside academics** — 学术之外  
**stay active** — 保持运动  
**go to the gym** — 去健身房  
**maintain a good balance between A and B** — 在 A 和 B 之间保持良好平衡

## 4. Grammar & Usage

### Besides + noun

> Besides research, I enjoy...

### started + -ing + time expression

> I started skiing in college.

### help someone + verb

> These activities help me maintain a good balance.

## 5. Natural Spoken English

Outside research, I try to stay pretty active. I like basketball, and I started skiing when I was in college. I also go to the gym and walk quite a lot. On the technical side, I enjoy working on small projects and keeping my GitHub organized.

## 6. Expression Upgrade

**Basic**

> My hobby is basketball.

**Better**

> Outside research, I like staying active, especially through basketball, skiing, and going to the gym.
