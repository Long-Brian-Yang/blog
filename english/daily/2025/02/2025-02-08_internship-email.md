# 2025-02-08 — Internship Email: Professional Reply

> **Focus:** professional email · preparation · expressing interest

## Model Expressions

> **I will review the information carefully and prepare accordingly.**

> **It is a fascinating and challenging topic, and I look forward to discussing it with you.**

## 中文理解

这两句话解决正式邮件中很常见的两个任务：表达“我会认真看材料并做好准备”，以及“我对这个主题感兴趣，也期待进一步交流”。

## Vocabulary & Chunks

**review the information carefully** — 仔细查看信息  
**prepare accordingly** — 根据情况做好相应准备  
**a fascinating and challenging topic** — 有趣且具有挑战性的主题  
**look forward to discussing...** — 期待讨论……

## Grammar & Usage

**look forward to + noun / -ing**

> I look forward to **discussing** it with you.

不是 *look forward to discuss*。

**prepare accordingly** 中 *accordingly* 表示“根据前述信息采取相应行动”。

## Natural Spoken English

Thanks for sending the information. I'll go through it carefully and make sure I'm prepared. The topic looks really interesting, and I'm looking forward to talking about it in more detail.

## Active Output

1. 我会仔细查看资料并做好相应准备。
2. 我很期待进一步讨论这个主题。
