# 2025-02-02 — Fujitsu Interview: Self-Introduction & Personal PR

> **Focus:** interview presentation · motivation · personality · research identity

## 1. Presentation Logic

A clear interview introduction can follow:

**Who I am → What I have done → What I learned → Why this opportunity fits me**

The goal is not to repeat the CV. It is to give the interviewer a coherent picture of your personality, research path, and motivation.

## 2. Model English

Hello, my name is Long Yang. My background is in data science, and my current research combines machine learning with computational materials science.

During my undergraduate studies, I worked on deep-learning-based medical-image analysis. That experience trained me to learn independently and solve technical problems even when I entered a topic with limited prior knowledge.

In graduate school, I moved into materials science and began working on neural-network potentials and molecular-dynamics simulations. This transition required me to build a new scientific background while continuing to use my strengths in machine learning and programming.

I would describe myself as someone who enjoys learning across fields and turning unfamiliar problems into structured tasks that I can work through step by step.

This is also why I am interested in the Fujitsu Research Computing Lab. I am attracted to research environments where advanced computing, AI, and scientific problems come together, and I would like to learn how these technologies are developed and applied in an industrial research setting.

## 3. 中文理解

Personal PR 不应该只是“我很努力、我很认真”。更好的逻辑是用真实经历证明特点：

**数据科学 → 医学影像 → 自主学习能力 → 转入材料科学 → 跨领域学习 → 为什么 Fujitsu Research Computing Lab 与自己的路径契合。**

## 4. Key Vocabulary & Chunks

**my background is in...** — 我的背景是……  
**combine A with B** — 把 A 与 B 结合  
**limited prior knowledge** — 先验知识有限  
**move into a field** — 转入某领域  
**build a new scientific background** — 建立新的科学背景  
**strengths in...** — ……方面的优势  
**learn across fields** — 跨领域学习  
**turn a problem into structured tasks** — 把问题拆成结构化任务  
**work through something step by step** — 一步步解决  
**be attracted to...** — 被……吸引  
**industrial research setting** — 企业研究环境

## 5. Grammar & Usage

### This is why...

把经历与动机自然连接：

> I enjoy interdisciplinary research. **This is why** I am interested in the lab.

### where + clause

> a research environment **where advanced computing, AI, and scientific problems come together**

### describe myself as someone who...

比简单说 *I am hardworking* 更自然：

> I would describe myself as someone who enjoys learning across fields.

## 6. Natural Spoken English

My background is originally in data science, but over time I've moved more toward scientific applications of machine learning.

One thing that probably describes me well is that I'm comfortable entering a field I don't fully understand yet. I did that with deep learning during my undergraduate research, and then again when I moved into materials science.

I like breaking unfamiliar problems into smaller pieces and learning what I need along the way. That's a big reason why Fujitsu Research Computing Lab interests me — it sits right at the intersection of advanced computing, AI, and scientific research.

## 7. Expression Upgrade

**Basic**

> I like learning new things.

**Better**

> I'm comfortable entering unfamiliar fields and building the knowledge I need along the way.

**Basic**

> I want to join Fujitsu because it is a good company.

**Better**

> I'm attracted to the lab because it brings advanced computing, AI, and scientific research together in an industrial setting.
