# 2025-02-11 — Interview English: Explaining Past Research Experience

> **Focus:** technical interview · methods · personal contribution

## Reading 1 — Mathematical Modeling

In the 2019 Asia-Pacific Mathematical Modeling Contest, our team worked on analyzing regional economic vitality.

We used correlation analysis and time-series methods such as ARIMA and Holt-Winters, as well as machine-learning models including Random Forest and XGBoost. We also explored policy effects using difference-in-differences and Monte Carlo simulation.

My role involved data processing, model development, policy simulation, and report writing.

## Reading 2 — Medical-Image Segmentation

During my undergraduate research, I worked on lung-nodule segmentation from CT images.

The workflow included CT preprocessing and resampling, extracting 3D patches, training segmentation architectures such as U-Net and V-Net, and evaluating the models using metrics including Dice score, accuracy, recall, and F1 score.

This project gave me practical experience in deep learning, medical-image processing, model evaluation, and independent technical problem-solving.
