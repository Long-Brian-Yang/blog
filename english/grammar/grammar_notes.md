# Grammar & Usage Notes

This is not intended to be a complete grammar textbook. Add only grammar or usage points that appear naturally during reading and speaking practice.

## 001 — Comparisons and priorities

### care about whether + clause

Use this when the important point is whether something is true or works.

> A company may care more about **whether the method is reliable**.

### more valuable than + noun / gerund

> Delivering a reliable solution can be **more valuable than finding the theoretically best solution**.

### get used to + noun / -ing

> You **get used to dealing with uncertainty**.

Do not use *get used to deal with*.

### balance A with B

> We need to **balance scientific curiosity with practical constraints**.

## Principle

Prioritize grammar that improves real output. Avoid collecting rules that you already understand and use automatically.
