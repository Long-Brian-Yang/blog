# Speaking Notes

## Goal

Turn passive knowledge into **automatic spoken English**.

## Daily 10–20 minute routine

### 1. Shadowing — 3–5 min
Read a spoken passage aloud. Focus on rhythm, stress, and chunks rather than individual words.

### 2. Retelling — 3–5 min
Close the text and explain the same idea in your own words.

### 3. Free response — 3–5 min
Answer one open-ended question without writing a script first.

### 4. Upgrade — 3–5 min
Notice expressions that were difficult to produce and replace awkward wording with natural chunks.

## Fluency principle

Do not aim to speak as fast as possible. Speak in thought groups:

> I think a PhD helps a lot, /  
> but academia and industry /  
> can feel pretty different.

The target is **automatic phrasing**, not word-by-word translation.
